Senior Acoustic Engineer

La Jolla, CA Full-Time On-Site
Apply →

We're looking for a Senior Acoustic Engineer to join our driver development and tuning team. You'll work directly on the next generation of Velocity hardware — from transducer architecture to acoustic measurement and target curve development. This is a hands-on, highly technical role in a company where engineering decisions are made by engineers.

  • 5+ years of experience in acoustic transducer design for consumer or professional audio
  • Deep expertise in driver measurement, tuning, and frequency response optimization
  • Proficiency in acoustic simulation tools (COMSOL, ABEC, or equivalent)
  • Experience with perceptual evaluation methods and listening panel protocols
  • Familiarity with DSP fundamentals and how hardware and software interact at the driver level

Remote Full-Time
Apply →

The Velocity companion app is a critical part of the product experience. Working at Velocity

What to Expect

Small Team, Full Ownership

You won't be one of many on a large team. Everyone at Velocity owns a meaningful part of the product. Your decisions will ship.

High Standards, Low Bureaucracy

We move deliberately and ship things we're proud of. There's no approval theater — just clear expectations and the space to execute.

Competitive Compensation

We pay at or above market rate, offer equity, full health/dental/vision, and 20 days PTO. We're not asking you to bet on us — we're making a fair exchange.

Product Discount

Every Velocity employee receives the full product lineup at cost. We want the team to live with what they build.
