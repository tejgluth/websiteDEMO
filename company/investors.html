Overview

Company Overview

Velocity Audio, Inc. is a consumer electronics company founded in 2019 and headquartered in La Jolla, California. The company designs and sells the Velocity One ANC, a premium wireless over-ear headphone, direct-to-consumer through its own website.

Velocity's go-to-market strategy is built around direct distribution, which allows the company to maintain premium positioning while offering competitive pricing by eliminating traditional retail margins. The company does not sell through third-party retailers.

The Velocity One ANC is the company's first product and launched in Q4 2024. It is manufactured to Velocity's specifications at a contracted assembly facility in Japan, with drivers produced internally to proprietary design.

Market Context

Market Position

The global premium wireless headphone market exceeded $7 billion in 2024 and is projected to grow at a compound annual rate of approximately 12% through 2030, driven by remote work adoption, content consumption trends, and increasing consumer awareness of audio quality.

Velocity competes in the $300–$600 price segment — a segment that has historically been dominated by two or three large electronics conglomerates. The company's thesis is that direct-to-consumer distribution, focused engineering investment, and a single-product strategy allow a smaller company to deliver a technically superior product at a competitive price point.

Third-party technical reviews have confirmed that the Velocity One ANC achieves industry-leading noise cancellation attenuation and frequency response accuracy at its price point, validating the core product thesis.

Technology

Intellectual Property

Velocity Audio holds or has applied for patents covering the following areas of innovation:

  • Planar-hybrid transducer architecture for consumer headphones
  • Adaptive noise cancellation modeling using multi-microphone environmental classification
  • AXIS-3 processor power management for extended ANC runtime

The company's firmware and companion application software are proprietary. The AXIS-3 processor is designed to Velocity's specifications and manufactured by a contracted semiconductor partner; the company retains full ownership of the processor IP.
