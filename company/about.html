About Velocity

We build precision audio instruments for people who take sound seriously. Founded in 2019 in La Jolla, California.

Mission

Why Velocity Exists

Velocity Audio was founded in 2019 on a frustration shared by its founders: that exceptional sound quality had become an exclusive luxury, accessible only to those willing to spend several thousand dollars on audiophile gear — or accept meaningless compromises in mass-market products that prioritized marketing over engineering.

We believed it was possible to build a headphone that met the technical standards of professional monitoring equipment while remaining practical, comfortable, and affordable for the working professional, the serious commuter, and the musician on the road. The Velocity One ANC is the result of five years of development — and our first product.

Our founding team came from acoustic engineering backgrounds at a major professional audio manufacturer, a semiconductor company specializing in DSP for hearing devices, and an independent industrial design studio. We share a conviction that great sound should be achievable without compromise, and that the engineering required to get there is worth the investment.

Design Philosophy

How We Build

Every design decision at Velocity begins with an acoustic target, not an aesthetic one. We define the frequency response we want to achieve, the noise floor we intend to reach, and the driver architecture required to get there — then we design everything else around that foundation.

Materials are chosen for their acoustic and ergonomic properties first. The 6061 aluminum headband arc was selected because its stiffness-to-weight ratio allows us to maintain consistent clamping force across head sizes without resorting to a multi-piece hinge assembly that would introduce resonant frequencies. The 45kg/m³ memory foam in the earcushions was chosen after testing eleven densities for pressure distribution, acoustic seal quality, and breathability over extended wear.

We design for longevity. The Velocity One ANC is built to be repaired, not discarded. Earcushions are user-replaceable. The headband padding is removable. Replacement parts will be available for a minimum of 7 years from the product's release date.

Values

What We Stand For

01

Engineering Honesty

We publish real measurements and make only claims we can substantiate. Our specifications are tested under realistic conditions, not optimized test scenarios.

02

Repairability

We design products that can be maintained. Replacement parts will remain available for a minimum of 7 years. This is not a disposable product.

03

No-Nonsense Pricing

We sell direct. Our pricing reflects the cost of building a genuinely excellent product without the overhead of retail margins, influencer budgets, or celebrity endorsements.

04

Focused Product Range

We will not dilute our focus by releasing products we can't build to the standard Velocity One ANC established. One great product is worth more than a broad, mediocre lineup.
