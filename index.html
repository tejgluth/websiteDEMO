Product Overview

Clarity in Every Environment.

Designed for those who demand precision wherever they are. The Velocity One ANC is an over-ear headphone built around a custom 40mm planar-hybrid driver, tuned to deliver the accuracy of a studio monitor with the portability of an everyday companion.

Sound Engineering

Custom 40mm Planar-Hybrid Drivers.

Our driver architecture combines a planar membrane mid-range with a dynamic tweeter — eliminating the crossover frequency compromises that plague conventional designs. The result: a frequency response of 6Hz–42kHz with ±1.2dB accuracy across the audible range.

6Hz–42kHz Frequency Response
±1.2dB Accuracy
109dB SPL Sensitivity
34Ω Impedance

Noise Cancellation

Adaptive Noise Cancellation.

Six feedforward and feedback microphones work in concert with our proprietary AXIS-3 processor to model acoustic environments in real time. ANC depth adjusts automatically — deeper attenuation on the subway, lighter in open offices. Transparency mode passes ambient audio with less than 1ms latency.

32dB Max Attenuation
<1ms Transparency Latency
6 Microphones

Materials & Comfort

Precision-Engineered Materials.

The headband is formed from a single arc of 6061 aircraft-grade aluminum, finished with matte Type III anodization. Earcushions use 60-layer slow-recovery memory foam at 45kg/m³ density, covered in perforated protein leather. Clamping force is calibrated at 3.8N — firm enough for a secure fit, gentle enough for 8+ hours of wear.

Engineering

The AXIS-3 Processor.

Developed in-house over three years, the AXIS-3 is a 6-core DSP running at 768MHz. It handles ANC modeling, transparency processing, adaptive EQ, wear detection, auto-pause, and multipoint switching — all simultaneously — while consuming under 8mW. It's the reason we can offer 28 hours of active noise cancellation.
