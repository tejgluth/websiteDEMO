Topic 01

Getting Started

How do I power on the Velocity One ANC for the first time?

Press and hold the power button on the right earcup for 3 seconds. You'll hear a tone and the LED indicator will pulse blue, indicating the headphone is in pairing mode. On first power-on, the device automatically enters pairing mode — open Bluetooth settings on your phone or computer and select "Velocity One ANC" from the device list.

What does each button and gesture do?

Power / Pairing: Right earcup rear button. Hold 3s to power on/off; hold 6s to enter pairing mode.

ANC / Transparency: Right earcup front button. Single press cycles: ANC → Transparency → Off.

Playback: Right earcup touch surface. Tap to play/pause; swipe forward to skip; swipe back to previous.

Volume: Right earcup. Swipe up to increase, swipe down to decrease.

Calls: Single tap to answer/end; double tap to reject; hold for Siri/Google Assistant.

How do I download the Velocity companion app?

Search "Velocity Audio" in the App Store (iOS 15+) or Google Play Store (Android 10+). The app is free. Once installed, open the app with your headphone powered on and nearby — it will detect the headphone automatically and walk you through setup. From the app you can adjust ANC depth, transparency level, EQ presets, rename paired devices, configure wear detection, and manage firmware updates.

Is there a quick-start guide in the box?

Yes. A printed quick-start card is included. The full user manual is available in the companion app under Settings → Documentation, and as a downloadable PDF at velocityaudio.com/support. The manual covers advanced features including multipoint pairing, custom EQ setup, and firmware update procedures.

Topic 02

Connectivity

How do I pair to a new device?

With the headphone powered on, hold the power button for 6 seconds until you hear two ascending tones and the LED flashes blue rapidly — this indicates pairing mode. Open Bluetooth settings on your device and select "Velocity One ANC." If the headphone was previously paired to another device, it will attempt to reconnect to the last known device first; hold for 6s to override this.

How does multipoint Bluetooth work?

The Velocity One ANC supports simultaneous connections to up to 4 Bluetooth devices. Once paired to multiple devices, the headphone maintains active connections with all of them. When audio starts on a secondary device, it automatically switches or mixes depending on your multipoint mode setting in the app. You can name each connection and set priority order in the companion app under Connections.

Can I use the headphone wired?

Yes. The included 1.2m braided 3.5mm cable connects to the 3.5mm jack on the left earcup. Wired mode bypasses Bluetooth entirely, providing under 2ms latency. The headphone can be used wired even when the battery is fully depleted. In wired mode, the onboard AXIS-3 processor remains active for ANC and transparency if the battery has charge; otherwise passive listening is available.

Topic 03

Battery & Charging

How long does a full charge take?

A full charge from empty takes approximately 90 minutes using USB-C. The headphone supports fast charge: just 8 minutes of charging provides approximately 4 hours of playback. Charging is indicated by the LED on the right earcup: pulsing amber while charging, solid white when full. You can also view the battery percentage in the companion app or ask your phone's Bluetooth battery indicator.

Why is my battery life shorter than rated?

Rated battery life is measured at 75% volume, room temperature, with ANC at its default setting. Real-world usage can vary based on volume level, ANC depth, transparency mode, active multipoint connections, and ambient temperature. If you're regularly seeing dramatically lower battery life (under 20 hours with ANC off), contact support — battery degradation within the warranty period is covered.

Can I charge while listening?

Yes. Connect USB-C while listening over Bluetooth or wired — the headphone will charge and operate simultaneously. Note that heat generated during simultaneous charging and high-volume use may slightly reduce long-term battery capacity over time; for best longevity, charge when the battery is below 20% rather than keeping it continuously plugged in.

What happens when the battery is critically low?

At 10% battery, the headphone will announce the battery level via voice prompt every 15 minutes. At 3%, ANC and transparency mode are automatically disabled to extend remaining playback time. At battery exhaustion, the headphone powers off. You can still use it wired in passive mode with a depleted battery.

Topic 04

Audio Quality

What audio codecs does the Velocity One ANC support?

The Velocity One ANC supports SBC (universal), AAC (recommended for Apple devices), and LDAC (high-resolution, up to 990kbps). LDAC is the preferred codec for Android devices when high-quality wireless audio is the priority. AAC provides the best compatibility and latency balance for iOS. Codec priority is managed automatically, but you can force a codec in the companion app under Audio → Codec Preference.

How do I adjust the EQ?

Open the Velocity companion app and navigate to Sound → EQ. You can choose from five curated presets (Flat, Studio, Acoustic, Bass, Vocal) or create a fully custom 10-band EQ profile. Custom profiles are stored on-device and apply even when the app is closed. The headphone ships with a flat frequency response — presets are applied on top of this foundation.

My audio sounds muffled or one-sided. What should I check?

First, confirm the 3.5mm cable (if wired) is fully seated in both the headphone and source device. For Bluetooth: forget the device from Bluetooth settings, power-cycle the headphone, and re-pair. Check for EQ settings that may cause imbalance in the app. If one driver is silent, try a factory reset (hold power + ANC button simultaneously for 10 seconds). If the issue persists, contact support — driver failure is covered under warranty.
