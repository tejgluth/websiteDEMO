Shipping & Returns

Free shipping on every order. Straightforward returns. No restocking fees, no questions asked within 30 days.

Shipping

Delivery Options

All orders ship from our fulfillment center in San Diego, California. In-stock orders placed before 2pm PT on business days ship the same day. Orders placed after 2pm or on weekends ship the next business day.

Standard Shipping 3–5 business days  ·  Free
Express Shipping Next business day  ·  $15
Order cutoff 2:00 PM PT (business days)
Tracking Emailed within 1 hour of dispatch

You'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link as soon as your order ships. If you haven't received a tracking email within 2 business days of ordering, check your spam folder or contact us at support.

Returns

30-Day Return Window

If you're not completely satisfied with your Velocity One ANC, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund — no questions asked. We want you to love this product. If you don't, we'll make it right.

Return Conditions

To qualify for a full refund, returned items must meet the following conditions:

  • → Item must be returned in its original packaging with all included accessories
  • → Headphone, cables, carry case, and documentation must all be present
  • → No physical damage caused by the customer (beyond normal unboxing)
  • → Return must be initiated within 30 days of the delivery date on file

Items returned with missing accessories or excessive wear may be subject to a partial refund. We'll always communicate before making any deduction.

Process

How to Start a Return

Starting a return takes less than 2 minutes. Here's how:

01 — Contact Us

Email support@velocityaudio.com or use the contact form with your order number and the reason for your return.

02 — Receive Label

We'll email you a prepaid return shipping label within 1 business day. No postage cost to you.

03 — Ship It Back

Pack the headphone securely in the original box and drop it off at any authorized carrier location.

04 — Refund Issued

Once we receive and inspect the return, your refund is processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

Refunds

Refund Timeline

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the return. You'll receive an email confirmation the moment your refund is issued.

Credit card refunds may take an additional 2–5 business days to appear on your statement depending on your bank. PayPal refunds typically appear within 24 hours. If you haven't received your refund after 10 business days from our confirmation email, contact your bank or reach out to us.

International

International Shipping

At this time, the Velocity One ANC ships to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and select European Union countries. International shipping rates and timelines are calculated at checkout.

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, or VAT applicable in their country. Velocity does not collect or remit international taxes. Please check your local import regulations before ordering.

International returns follow the same 30-day policy; however, customers are responsible for return shipping costs from outside the United States. We recommend using a tracked service.
