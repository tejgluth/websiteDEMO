Warranty

The Velocity One ANC is backed by a 2-year limited warranty. We build products to last — and we stand behind them.

Coverage

What's Covered

Velocity Audio, Inc. warrants the Velocity One ANC against defects in materials and workmanship for a period of two (2) years from the original date of purchase. This warranty covers:

  • ◎ Manufacturing defects in the headphone, earcups, headband, and wiring
  • ◎ Driver failure or significant degradation in acoustic performance
  • ◎ ANC or transparency mode hardware malfunction not caused by user damage
  • ◎ USB-C charging port failure under normal use
  • ◎ Battery capacity falling below 70% of rated capacity within 2 years under normal use
  • ◎ AXIS-3 processor or Bluetooth module failure

Warranty coverage applies to the original purchaser and is non-transferable. Proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt) is required when filing a claim.

Exclusions

What's Not Covered

The following are not covered under the limited warranty:

Accidental damage Drops, impacts, crushing, or damage from improper storage
Liquid damage The Velocity One ANC is not rated for water resistance. Exposure to rain, sweat, or liquid voids the warranty
Unauthorized modification Any alteration, repair, or modification performed by a party other than Velocity Audio or an authorized service center
Cosmetic wear Normal wear to earcushions, finish, or cables from regular use
Accessories Included cables and carry case are not covered by the hardware warranty
Third-party damage Damage caused by use of non-approved accessories, chargers above 18W, or third-party software
Claims

How to File a Warranty Claim

Warranty claims are handled directly by Velocity Audio. There are no third-party service centers — you deal with us directly for a faster, more consistent experience.

01 — Email Warranty Support

Contact warranty@velocityaudio.com with your order number, a description of the issue, and photos or video if applicable.

02 — Diagnosis

Our technical team will review your claim within 2 business days and may request additional information or a remote diagnostic through the companion app.

03 — Resolution

Approved claims are resolved by repair, replacement with a factory-refurbished unit, or replacement with a new unit at Velocity's discretion.

04 — Shipping

We cover all shipping costs for valid warranty claims within the United States. A prepaid label will be emailed to you.

Warranty claims are typically resolved within 7–10 business days of us receiving the unit.
