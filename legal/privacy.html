Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2025. This policy explains how Velocity Audio, Inc. collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

1. What We Collect

When you interact with Velocity Audio, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity and Contact Information: Name, email address, shipping address, billing address, and phone number provided at the time of purchase or account registration.
  • Transaction Data: Details of products ordered, purchase amounts, payment method type (not full card numbers — payment processing is handled by a PCI-compliant third-party processor), order history, and return records.
  • Device and Technical Data: When you use our website, we automatically collect IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and time spent on pages through server logs and analytics tools.
  • Companion App Data: If you use the Velocity companion app, we may collect device identifiers, headphone firmware version, usage statistics (e.g., feature use frequency), crash reports, and EQ preferences. Audio content is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Communications: Records of your communications with our support team, including email correspondence and support tickets.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as government identification numbers, health information, or financial account details beyond payment method type.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Order Fulfillment: Processing and shipping your orders, providing order status updates, and managing returns and warranty claims.
  • Customer Support: Responding to your inquiries, troubleshooting technical issues, and processing warranty requests.
  • Product Improvement: Anonymized and aggregated usage data from the companion app is used to improve firmware, software features, and future product development. Individual usage data is not shared.
  • Legal Compliance: Complying with applicable laws and regulations, including tax obligations and consumer protection requirements.
  • Fraud Prevention: Detecting and preventing fraudulent transactions and unauthorized account access.
  • Communications: With your consent, sending transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping notifications) and, separately, marketing emails about new products or promotions. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising targeting on third-party platforms.

3. Third Parties

We share your information only with the following categories of service providers who support our operations, and only to the extent necessary to perform their services:

  • Payment Processors: We use a PCI DSS-compliant payment processor to handle all payment card transactions. We do not store full credit card numbers.
  • Shipping Carriers: Your name and address are shared with our shipping partners to fulfill your order and provide tracking.
  • Analytics Providers: We use privacy-focused website analytics to understand site traffic. Analytics data is anonymized prior to transmission.
  • Email Service Provider: We use a third-party email platform to send transactional and marketing emails. Email addresses are used solely for this purpose.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Our website and backend systems are hosted on reputable cloud infrastructure providers operating under data processing agreements.

All service providers are contractually required to handle your data in accordance with this privacy policy and applicable law. We do not permit service providers to use your data for their own purposes.

4. Your Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Know: You may request a summary of the personal information we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you in a portable format.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain exceptions (e.g., ongoing legal obligations).
  • Right to Correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information, so this right does not apply; however, you may contact us to confirm this in writing.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

California residents may exercise their rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) by contacting us at privacy@velocityaudio.com. Residents of the European Economic Area may exercise their rights under the GDPR by contacting us at the same address.

We respond to all verified privacy requests within 30 days. Identity verification may be required before processing a request.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Specifically:

  • Order and Transaction Records: Retained for 7 years to comply with tax and accounting obligations.
  • Support Communications: Retained for 3 years from the close of the support interaction.
  • Marketing Consent Records: Retained until opt-out plus 1 year for compliance purposes.
  • Website Analytics: Retained in anonymized form for up to 2 years.

When retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including TLS encryption for data in transit, encrypted storage for sensitive data at rest, access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel, and regular security reviews. No transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your information.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website and products are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page and notify customers who have opted into email communications. Continued use of our services after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
